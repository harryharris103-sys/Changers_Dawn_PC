v0.5.0 
Weapons: 24 slots (13 new)

Replacements: The AK-Dawn is now the AK-12, and the old shotgun is now your supplied shotgun model. The shotgun is a single piece, so it no longer has a pump animation on firing.
New rifles: AK-19, Colt M4A1, Colt M5 SCW, Honey Badger, ADAR 2-15, DD4 X1, FN M16A4, UAR-15 S1, HK MR762, M110 SASS and UAR-10.
Others: FN P90 and a Magnum revolver.
Model handling: I simplified each model, aimed it forward and moved the magazine out as its own piece. I also placed hands and sights per weapon, and the scoped M110 and UAR-10 get the scope overlay.
Stats: All stats on the new guns are my first guesses.

Switching weapons

Class keys: Keys 1-5 now pick a class: Assault, SMG, Marksman, Shotgun and Pistol. Pressing the same key again cycles through that class.
Q and wheel: Q and the mouse wheel still cycle all 24.
HUD and loot: A strip at the bottom shows the current class. Battle Royale loot can now be any of the 24.

Reload animations

Method: I pulled the motion out of your animation files. Hand paths, magazine paths and gun movement are scaled to each weapon and stretched to its reload time.
Magazines: On models with a separate magazine, you see it drop out and go back in.
Empty reloads: Carbine-style guns do a longer reload when the gun is empty.
Who uses which:
M4 pack: carbines and most SMGs.
M4A1 clip: the AKs and P90.
FAL clip: the battle rifles.
Shotgun clip: the shotgun.
Pistols and Magnum: a simple motion I made myself, since none of the files had one.
Older models: The earlier weapons (Kriss, MP7, MP5, SCAR, M4A1, M16 and the pistols) have hands and gun motion but no moving magazine.
