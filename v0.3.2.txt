The polish is published as v0.3.2 at the same link.

Soldiers

Pose: They now hold their rifles at the shoulder with both hands, with the rifle pointing forward. Before, the arms hung at their sides and the rifle floated sideways.
Helmets and gear: I added ear guards, a helmet mount, a neck guard and a bedroll on the backpack. More pouches sit on the chest, plus forearm guards, thigh pouches and boot soles.
Rifles: Each now has an optic and a muzzle brake. The Recon gets a long scope with a glowing lens, and the Heavy gets a bulky barrel shroud and an ammo box. The Commander's rifle has a gold stripe.
Per character: The Commander has gold epaulets, gold chest trim and a radio antenna. The Medic has red shoulder bands. The Heavy has stacked shoulder armor and a second chest plate. The Recon has hanging camo strands on the shoulders.

Monsters

Stalker: It now has ribs.
Brute: Back plates and shoulder spikes.
Ravager: A segmented tail.
Crawler: Its legs now reach down to the ground as pointed legs.
Alpha: A glowing neck frill and shoulder spikes.
Teeth: Most of the monsters now have teeth.

Lighting and effects

Shading: Models are now shaded darker toward the ground, on undersides and around the feet, so they look less flat.
Muzzle flashes: Bots flash when they fire, so firefights are easier to read.
Movement: Soldiers bob a little as they walk, and monsters sway.

The models are still blocky, since this is polish on the same low-detail blockouts.
